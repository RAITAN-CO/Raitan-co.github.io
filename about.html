About Raitan

Singapore's Specialist in Custom Battery Engineering

Raitan is a Singapore-based engineering firm specialising in custom battery systems, solar energy solutions, and subsea engineering. We design and assemble every system to customer specification — no off-the-shelf products, no compromises.

Who we are

Raitan Pte Ltd is a Singapore-incorporated engineering firm founded in 2022 — custom battery systems across LFP, NMC, and other chemistries, solar energy solutions including BIPV, and subsea engineering support.

No catalogue products. Every system — a 300m-rated subsea pack, a carpark-rooftop ESS, a high-current EV pack — is engineered and assembled to the customer's exact spec at our facility at 20 Woodlands Link, Singapore.

Active member of Singapore's battery standards workgroups — SS725, TR77, TR136.

Company philosophy

Longevity is sustainability. A battery that lasts 3,000 cycles doesn't reach landfill — we engineer for lifespan, cutting replacement frequency and total cost of ownership.

Precision over standard. Every system is specified exactly for the application — no over- or under-engineering. Customers pay for the performance they need, nothing more.

Closing the green energy loop. Land-scarce Singapore has no room for conventional solar. Our BIPV generates power from surfaces that already exist — floors, walkways, terraces — paired with MUGEN storage in dead space.
