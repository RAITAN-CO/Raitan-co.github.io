Design for dead spaces

MUGEN is a modular battery pack engineered to stack and distribute across a building's underutilised areas — carpark corners, rooftop plant spaces, and other dead zones conventional large-format BESS can't use.

Packs connect in series to 500VDC nominal, and in parallel without limit — scaling with demand over time without replacing existing hardware. A PV input accepts up to 1000VDC / 15kW, pairing naturally with Raitan's solar pavement and solar deck installations.

Key benefits

Cost saving. Curbs contracted capacity costs and land opportunity cost by deploying into otherwise unusable space.

Safety by design. Distributed topology reduces risk concentration and prevents fire propagation between cells.

Space maximisation. No dedicated plant room — packs install into carpark pillars, ceiling voids, rooftop corners.

Peak shaving. Discharge at peak demand to reduce contracted grid capacity and monthly utility bills.
