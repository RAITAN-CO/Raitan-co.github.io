Solar solutions

Building-Integrated PV

Singapore doesn't have enough open land or roof space for conventional solar. Raitan's solar pavement and deck systems, with Platio, generate power from surfaces already part of your built environment.

Pilot deployment: Sentosa Skywalk

Raitan completed a 612Wp pilot installation at Sentosa Skywalk, integrating photovoltaic paving with micro-inverter technology — each tile operates independently, so moving shadows from walkway railings don't degrade the whole array.

Recognised at the 2022 Sustainability Open Innovation Challenge, winning the Sentosa Challenge category.
