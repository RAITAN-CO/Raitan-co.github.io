High current battery pack

TAKI Series

Built for electric motor vehicles, AGVs, and high-demand industrial drives. 24V and 48V variants, NMC / LFP / NA chemistry, integrated high-current BMS.

Design for high current

Where conventional packs throttle output under load to protect cells, TAKI's BMS and cell configuration are engineered from first principles around peak discharge performance.

Built-to-order — voltage, capacity, form factor, cell count, BMS thresholds, and connector type are all specifiable at enquiry.

Applications

Electric motor vehicles. Golf carts, utility vehicles, last-mile delivery EVs, and light electric transport.

Industrial drives & equipment. Forklifts, scissor lifts, powered industrial tools — the 48V variant covers a full shift without interim charging.

Defence & specialist platforms. Custom voltage, form factor, connector, and BMS for ground vehicles, UAV ground support, and portable power. Defence enquiries handled in confidence.
