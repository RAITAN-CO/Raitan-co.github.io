Underwater battery pack

SEN Series

Depth-rated lithium battery systems for AUV, ROV, and subsea robotic platforms. Three energy configurations, rated to 300m.

Product overview

The SEN Series is Raitan's underwater battery platform — pressure-rated, cylindrical lithium packs built for AUVs, ROVs, and subsea robotics down to 300 metres.

All SEN batteries use UN 38.3 compliant cells, assembled under Raitan's ISO 9001:2015 QMS. Each unit includes an integrated BMS with cell-level protection, RS485 telemetry, and SOC reporting to topside systems.

Applications

Underwater vehicles. AUV propulsion and hotel load for survey, inspection, and defence missions. Multiple units wire in parallel for extended endurance. Voltage, housing, depth rating, and connectors are customisable.

Subsea instruments & sensors. Long-duration power for seabed monitoring nodes, acoustic modems, and environmental sensors.
